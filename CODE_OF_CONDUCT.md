# Code of Conduct

forgecpp follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, and assuming good intent;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code and ideas;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers: through the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** reaches the
maintainers privately, and may be used for conduct reports too), or by
contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Every report is
reviewed and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
